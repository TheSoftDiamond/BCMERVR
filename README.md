# BCMER VR
![Screenshot](https://softdiamond.net/BCMERAssets/vrbcmer2.png)

- BCMER has a website! You can find it at [https://bcmer.softdiamond.net/](https://bcmer.softdiamond.net/)
- BCMER has a API! You can find it at [https://bcmerapi.softdiamond.net/](https://bcmerapi.softdiamond.net/)

Note: This mod is only a tool that helps bridge the gap between VR and BCMER. Whilst this mod is not required to experience or play BCMER with VR, this mod does a few changes that help the experience and make it a little more friendly to those playing with VR. In its current state, some settings may not work as expected and may lead to potential crashes or unintended behavior.
